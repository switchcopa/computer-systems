
Practice Problem 4.10 (solution page 520)
Suppose you want to implement a word-level equality circuit using the exclusive-
or circuits from Problem 4.9 rather than from bit-level equality circuits. Design
such a circuit for a 64-bit word consisting of 64 bit-level exclusive-or circuits and
two additional logic gates.

Let's make this equality circuit. First, we need 2 64-bit input words A and B
layed down sequentially:

A_0     B_0
A_1     B_1
     .
     .
     .
A_63    B_63

We would wire a single-bit XOR gate to every single pair (A_i, B_i),
we would get a new 64-bit word that has each bit i set if A_i and B_i
differ, otherwise zero.

Then, we would feed that result to a single
word 64-bit OR, that would give us a bool signal 0 if every pair is
identical, and 1 otherwise. The way it works is that it combines the
bit signals in an internal tree structure that has 16 4-input OR gates,
then 8 4-input OR gates, then 4, feeding into one final 4-input OR gate.

Lastly, feed that signal to a NOT gate to get the desired 64-bit
comparator.
